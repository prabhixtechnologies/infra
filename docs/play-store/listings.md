## MobiStack (`app.prabhix.fixflow`)

- **Title:** MobiStack
- **Short description (max 80):** Shop inventory, sales, repairs, and job tickets for mobile-repair businesses.
- **Full description:**
  MobiStack is the shop floor app for mobile-repair businesses on Prabhix. Track inventory and parts, take in devices, run job tickets, record sales, and keep the counter and back room in sync.

  Sign in with your Prabhix account. Data stays in your organisation. Camera is used only to scan barcodes.

  A Prabhix product. Requires an active MobiStack workspace.

- **Category:** Business
- **Tags:** inventory, repairs, POS, job tickets

## Prabhix OneOps (`com.prabhix.operator`)

- **Title:** Prabhix OneOps
- **Short description:** Operations console on the phone — visitors, live ops, and team chat.
- **Full description:**
  Prabhix OneOps is the operator app for organisations running on Prabhix. See who is on site, handle visitor flow, open the ops dashboard, and continue work chat from your phone.

  Sign in with Prabhix Identity (secure browser sign-in, no password stored in the app).

  For members of a Prabhix organisation. Not a consumer social app.

- **Category:** Business

## Prabhix Mailroom (`com.prabhix.mailroom`)

- **Title:** Prabhix Mailroom
- **Short description:** Shared mailbox and helpdesk threads for your organisation.
- **Full description:**
  Prabhix Mailroom puts your organisation’s shared inbox on Android. Read threads, reply, and keep support mail with the rest of your Prabhix workspace.

  Sign in with Prabhix Identity. Mail content belongs to your organisation.

- **Category:** Business

## Prabhix Admin (`com.prabhix.admin`) — Production (staff-only listing)

- **Title:** Prabhix Admin
- **Short description:** Platform overview for Prabhix staff (tenants, pipeline, logs).
- **Full description:**
  Prabhix Admin is a staff-only console for Prabhix Technologies. It is not intended for public end users. Access is limited to invited platform operators.

- **Category:** Business
- **Distribution:** Public Production requested 2026-09-21. Play still requires a closed test (12 testers / 14 days) before Production can be applied for. Listing is staff-only copy; access remains Identity-gated.

## Graphics to upload

| File | Play field |
|---|---|
| `assets/<app>-icon-512.png` | High-res icon 512×512 |
| `assets/<app>-feature-1024x500.png` | Feature graphic 1024×500 |
| Phone screenshots (you take these) | At least 2 JPEG/PNG, 16:9 or 9:16 |

`<app>` is `mobistack`, `oneops`, `mailroom`, or `admin`.
